Your information is personal. Here's plainly what protects it — and what you can do.

🔐

Encrypted connection

Everything between your phone and the portal travels over HTTPS, so others on the same Wi-Fi can't read it.

🔑

Passwords are never stored in plain text

Sign-in is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication. Not even the admins can see your password.

🛡️

Forced new password

If you sign in with the default password you must replace it before using anything else.

🗂️

Private vs public data

Your full record (NIC, contacts, medical notes) is kept separate from the small public directory card others can see.

🚧

Rules enforced on the server

Access isn't just hidden in the app — the database itself refuses requests you aren't allowed to make, even if someone tries to bypass the app.

👥

Limited admin powers

Each admin only gets the specific permissions they need (e.g. Fund or Events). Only super admins can add or change admins.

🙈

You control your visibility

Hide your profile details, and others see only your level — never exact points or donations.

✍️

Approved changes only

Edits to your official details go through an admin approval, and protected fields can't be changed by anyone through the app.

Being honest about limits: a few trusted admins can view private details when their role needs it. Your data is stored with Google Firebase. No system is 100% unbreakable — which is why your own habits below matter.

Your part: 8 habits that keep you safe

  • Use a password that's unique to this portal — not your NIC, birthday or phone number.
  • Never share your password or screenshots showing it. Admins will never ask for it.
  • Only enter your details at japurafood.edu.lk. Check the address before you sign in.
  • Sign out (menu → Log out) on shared or borrowed devices and computers.
  • Lock your phone with a PIN or fingerprint.
  • Think before posting personal details on Idea Boards or in requests — the batch can read them.
  • Notice something odd (unknown activity, wrong info on your profile)? Tell an admin straight away.
  • Keep your browser and phone updated.
🚩 Spotting a fake: messages with links to other websites asking you to “verify your portal account”, or anyone asking for your password or a code, are scams. Don't click — report it to an admin.

11Help & FAQ

Quick answers.

I forgot my password

Ask a batch admin to reset it, then sign in and choose a new password when prompted.

I'm not getting notifications

Open Settings → turn Push notifications on, and allow notifications for the site in your browser/phone settings. After the move to the new address you need to allow them once again.

My details are wrong

Go to Settings → Request a detail change. An admin reviews and approves it.

I can't mark attendance

Attendance can only be marked on the day of the lecture, and only for lectures that are scheduled.

The page looks broken or old

Refresh the page. If it persists, clear the site data in your browser or reinstall the home-screen shortcut.

Who do I contact?

Message any batch admin or send an admin-only request from the Requests page.
